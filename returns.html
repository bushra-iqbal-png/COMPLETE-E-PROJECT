Returns & Exchanges Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, please review our Returns & Exchanges Policy below.

Return Eligibility

  • Items can be returned within 7 days of receiving the order.
  • Products must be unused, unworn, and in their original condition.
  • All original tags, packaging, and accessories must be included.
  • Proof of purchase or order number is required.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Used or damaged products.
  • Customized or personalized items.
  • Items purchased on clearance or final sale.

Exchange Policy

Customers may request an exchange for a different size, color, or replacement product within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock availability.

Return Process

  • Contact our customer support team.
  • Provide your order number and reason for return.
  • Follow the return instructions provided by our team.
  • Ship the item back in its original packaging.

Refunds

After receiving and inspecting the returned product, refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days using the original payment method.

Return Shipping Costs

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item received is defective, damaged, or incorrect.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Contact Us

For any questions regarding returns or exchanges, please contact our customer support team.
