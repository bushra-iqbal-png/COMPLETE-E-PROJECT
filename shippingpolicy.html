Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with us. We are committed to delivering your order safely and on time. Please read our shipping policy carefully.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1-3 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Delivery Time

  • Local Delivery: 3-5 business days
  • Nationwide Delivery: 5-7 business days
  • Remote Areas: 7-10 business days

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size. Special promotions may include free shipping on selected products.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email or SMS to monitor your shipment status.

Delivery Delays

Unexpected circumstances such as weather conditions, public holidays, or courier issues may cause delivery delays. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information. We are not responsible for delays or lost packages caused by incorrect addresses.

Damaged or Lost Packages

If your package arrives damaged or is lost during transit, please contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery for assistance.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding shipping, please contact our customer support team.
